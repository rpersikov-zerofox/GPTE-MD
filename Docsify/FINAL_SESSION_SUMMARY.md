## Writing Improvements Applied

### Content Quality

1. **Eliminated Non-Native Patterns**
   - "obtain" → "view/access/generate"
   - "decide on" → "select/choose"
   - "allow to [verb]" → "allow you to [verb]"

2. **Active Voice Throughout**
   - Before: "Invoices are issued by the system"
   - After: "The system issues invoices"

3. **Clear Headings**
   - Task-based ("Create a New User" not "Creating Users")
   - Scannable hierarchy
   - Descriptive and specific

### Structural Enhancements

1. **Overview Sections** - Every page explains purpose and scope
2. **Workflows** - Step-by-step processes for common tasks
3. **Prerequisites** - Clear requirements before procedures
4. **Troubleshooting** - Common issues and solutions
5. **Related Topics** - Navigation links to connected content
6. **Best Practices** - Guidance on optimal usage

### Special Formatting

- **Collapsible Sections** (`<details>`) for long lists (User Roles)
- **Proper Tables** - Converted broken grid tables to markdown
- **Consistent Lists** - Bullet lists for features, numbered for procedures
- **Clear Callouts** - Important notes and warnings highlighted
