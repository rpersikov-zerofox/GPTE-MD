# Blogs

The blog content type functionality helps you connect with your clients through an existing blog. To add a new blog entry, click on **Add New Blogs** and fill in the fields.

:::: note
::: title
Note
:::

It is necessary to fill in fields for every locale to display blogs to all possible locales on the booking engine.
::::

![Adding a New Blog Entry](/assets/Adding-New-Blog.png)

## Blog Page Customization

The CMS allows you to name and categorize blog pages, add images and URLs to content pages.

![Blog Page Customization](/assets/Page-Customization.jpg)

## Image Upload

To upload an image click **Add → Add more assets**. The added images will be uploaded into the media Library.

![Uploading an Image](/assets/Adding-Image.jpg)

![Browsing and Adding Assets](/assets/Browsing-Assets.jpg)

There are 2 ways to upload your image: from computer or from an URL.

![Uploading an Image](/assets/Image-Upload.png)

## Article Date

You can filter blogs by date. By default, the entries with the most recent article date are shown first.

## Display Configuration

To display your blog content on the booking engine, make sure that the **OnHomePage** field is switched ON

![OnHomePage Switch](/assets/OnHomePage-Switch.png)

Now the Blog will be displayed on your website:

![Blog Page Display](/assets/Blog-Page-Display.jpg)

You can go to the external blog page by clicking on the blog's picture.
