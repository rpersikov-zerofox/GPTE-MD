# Tips

Working with tourist products requires storing, tracking, and displaying important information regarding properties, transfers, excursions, and other products.

**GP Travel Enterprise** provides Tour Operators, Agency users, and Tourists with such functionality within the **Tips** module. Tips may be used for sharing with users different important information such as city guides, vaccination information or documents required information etc.

First, make sure the **Tips** module is turned on on your installation.

**Tips configuration is currently available via administration panel.**

## General Tips configuration

To create a new Tip,

1.  navigate to General settings menu by clicking on the Gear sign → select **Tips:**

![2024-11-26_12-25-43.jpg](/assets/2024-11-26_12-25-43.jpg)

1.  click on **Create** button →\* New tip\* window appears:

![2024-11-26_12-27-33.jpg](/assets/2024-11-26_12-27-33.jpg)

![2024-11-26_12-28-08.jpg](/assets/2024-11-26_12-28-08.jpg)

1.  fill in the form: 1. select the needed language and enter the title of the tip (ex. London Guide); 2. enter the description; 3. specify to whom the tip should be displayed (select the required user level by clicking on the corresponding radio button): \* within 'clients' you may specify the clients type to be displayed to, \* within sales channels you may select a specific partner. Just click add and select the required ones. 4. apply the conditions → select the product type the tip will be applied to (ex. For hotels). Click '+' and specify the conditions by selecting them from the drop-downs in the fields. For ex. Selecting 'City - equal - London' means the created tip will be displayed to those who will book the hotel in London:

![2024-11-26_13-58-04.jpg](/assets/2024-11-26_13-58-04.jpg)

Repeat the condition setting for other types of products in case required.

e.  Choose the type of document the tip will be displayed in (invoice or quotation);
f.  add an attachment by clicking **Upload a file** or via the link;
g.  click **Save**.

## Tips configuration on particular product level

**Tips** may be set not only on the product type level (such as hotels, transfers etc) but also on the particular product level (for ex. tip can be set for a specific hotel).

To create a new tip for a particular product,

1.  navigate to **Products** → select a product (ex. a hotel);
2.  open a Tips tab of the selected product, click **Create**:

![2024-11-26_14-04-19.jpg](/assets/2024-11-26_14-04-19.jpg)

1.  fill in the form: 1. Select the needed language and enter the title of the Tip (ex. Hotel rules). 2. Enter the description (ex. 'Smoking is prohibited in all rooms!'). 3. Specify to whom the tip should be displayed (select the required user level by clicking on the corresponding radio button). 4. You may apply the conditions in case needed: Click '+' and specify the conditions by selecting them from the drop-downs in the fields. For example, you may limit the time of the tip validity:

![2024-11-26_15-03-46.jpg](/assets/2024-11-26_15-03-46.jpg)

e.  For hotel services select the type of room the tip should be applied to.
f.  Choose the type of document the tip will be displayed in (invoice or quotation).
g.  Add an attachment in case needed.

<!-- -->

1.  Click **Save**. The rule is stored in the list:

![2024-11-26_15-09-28.jpg](/assets/2024-11-26_15-09-28.jpg)

## Tips displaying

To view the configured tips, initiate the search according to the indicated conditions and proceed to the booking.

The Tip is displayed on the booking for travelers along with the important information in the pop-up window. And the user may download all the necessary information:

![2024-11-26_15-12-47.jpg](/assets/2024-11-26_15-12-47.jpg)

In case a tip is configured on particular product level, it is displayed on the booking for travelers along with general tip information in the pop-up window:

![2024-11-26_12-25-43.jpg](/assets/2024-11-26_12-25-43.jpg)

Besides, Tips are displayed in Reservations:

- Proceed with the booking;
- open the reservation → scroll to the bottom: under the summary block on the right bottom corner of the page, the tip is displayed:

![2024-11-26_12-25-43.jpg](/assets/2024-11-26_12-25-43.jpg)

In case a tip is configured on particular product level, it can be viewed clicking on the tip icon near the service → the tip information is displayed in a popup window:

![2024-11-26_12-25-43.jpg](/assets/2024-11-26_12-25-43.jpg)

Tips are also displayed in the documents indicated in configuration (invoice or quotation).

![2024-11-26_12-25-43.jpg](/assets/2024-11-26_12-25-43.jpg)
