# Creating a holding as a separate legal entity

In case the department is not a company branch but a separate legal client (e.g. travel agency),

1.  In the Department window, select the **Client is a separate legal entity** check box. The form expands:

![2024-11-16_14-28-05.jpg](/assets/2024-11-16_14-28-05.jpg)

2.  Fill in the company information and click **Save**. The department is displayed in the list of company departments:

![2024-11-16_14-29-49.jpg](/assets/2024-11-16_14-29-49.jpg)
