# Adding a New Client

To register a new Agency please, do the following:

1.  On the Navigation menu click **My Company** →\*\*Agencies/Distributors\*\*.
2.  On the Agencies/Distributors tab, click **New Agency**. Create New Agency page appears.

![2024-10-29_11-57-42.jpg](/assets/2024-10-29_11-57-42.jpg)

1.  On the Create new client page, fill in the information about the client company as it is described in [Configuring Information about the Company](https://gp-team.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPTEUG/pages/1922236498). *The fields to be filled mandatory are marked with the asterisk (*)\*
2.  Click **Save**.

Note: Additional fields for Email addresses are added to the company profiles of Tour Operator 1level, Tour Operator 2level, Travel agency, Corporate client, Internal Supplier. You can use it if it\'s necessary to send Email notifications to several company email addresses.
