# Setting and Creating VAT in Client Contracts

**This functionality is currently available via administration panel.**

In case the legislation of your country presupposes adding VAT, you should configure VAT plans for settlements with clients. You can apply VAT on the end-price of the product or on the sum of commission.

To add VAT,

1.  Open the profile of the required client.
2.  Go to the Contract tab.
3.  On the VAT tab, select the option from the **End-price VAT**drop-dawn\*\*:\*\*

![image-20200922-093150.jpg](/assets/image-20200922-093150.jpg)

4.  Click **Save**.

**Creating a New VAT Plan**

In case there are no VAT plans available in the drop-downs, you should configure them separately.

To configure a VAT plan,

1.  On the Finance menu, click Payment settings.

![image-20200922-093302.jpg](/assets/image-20200922-093302.jpg)

2.  Go to the **VAT** tab.
3.  Click **Create**. The New VAT plan window appears:

![image-20200925-070511.jpg](/assets/image-20200925-070511.jpg)

4.  Enter the name of the VAT plan.
5.  Specify the VAT value. Additionally, you can differentiate VAT values depending on the product type, supplier, country, and group of tags.
6.  Click **Save** and then\*\* Close\*\* to finish the procedure.
