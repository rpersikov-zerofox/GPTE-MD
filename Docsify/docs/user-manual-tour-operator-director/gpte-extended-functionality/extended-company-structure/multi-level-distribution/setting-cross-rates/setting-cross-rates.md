# Setting Cross-rates

**This functionality is currently available via administration panel.**

On the Cross-rates tab you can set the increases to the exchange rates. The rates for products and services can be displayed in different currencies. You convert rates into single currency -- the one you use in your company as the accounting currency. For such conversions the system uses currency exchange rates established on the day of payment. According to the common practice a fixed percentage is added to these rates. **For example**, the exchange rate is EUR 1 = 0.95 USD. With a 3% increment to the rate it will be USD 0.9785. Locate mouse pointer into the respective boxes and specify the cross-rate for the currencies:
