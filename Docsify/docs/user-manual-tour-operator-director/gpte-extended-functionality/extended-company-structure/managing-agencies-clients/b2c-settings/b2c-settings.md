# B2C Settings

**This functionality is currently available via administration panel.**

You can create and change terms of direct sales for your clients.

To create a B2C-site for a client company,

1.  On the Clients menu click **Agencies/Distributors**.
2.  In the profile of a required client, go to the\*\* B2C Settings \*\*tab.
3.  On the B2C Settings tab, click **Create**. The New B2C window appears:

![image-20200925-072210.png](/assets/image-20200925-072210.png)

4.  Specify the required information as described in the Sales settings. (See [Configuring B2C Sales Settings](https://gp-team.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPTE/pages/895811597)).
5.  Click **Save** and then click **Close** to finish the procedure.
