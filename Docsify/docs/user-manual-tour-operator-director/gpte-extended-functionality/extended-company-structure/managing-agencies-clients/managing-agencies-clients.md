# Managing Agencies\' Clients

**This functionality is currently available via administration panel.**

Using system tools, you can create vast multilevel network of clients. On the **Clients** tab, you can browse the list of clients of your partner company. To browse the list of clients of an agency,

1.  On the Navigation bar, point to **Clients**, and click **Agencies/Distributors**.
2.  Click the agency of your choice.
3.  Click **Clients** to proceed to the Clients tab. On the Clients tab, you will see the list of agency\'s clients displayed.

Note that you can only view information about the customers located on top hierarchical levels of the agency\'s network. For instance, you will see the first-level tour operators -- those are the customers of your client agencies/distributors who purchase travel products and services directly from them.

If there is no data, it means that your partner agency/distributor does not have any agent network.
