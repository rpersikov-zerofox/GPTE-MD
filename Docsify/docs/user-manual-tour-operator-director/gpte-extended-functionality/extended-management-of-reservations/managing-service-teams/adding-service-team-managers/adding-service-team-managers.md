# Adding service team managers

To add a service team manager,

1.  Click **Add a manager**. The list of company users appears.

![image-20200924-114433.jpg](/assets/image-20200924-114433.jpg)

2.  Pick one or several users by selecting the check boxes.
3.  Click **Add**. The selected managers appear on the Service managers list. To delete one or several managers, click the Delete icon in the corresponding line.
