# Assigning a reservation to a Service Team

To assign a reservation to a particular service team,

1.  On the reservations menu click **View reservations**.
2.  Open the required reservation.
3.  In the Service group, opposite the Service team label, click **(Edit)**.

![image-20200924-115303.jpg](/assets/image-20200924-115303.jpg)

4.  The Select service team window appears.

![image-20200924-115359.jpg](/assets/image-20200924-115359.jpg)

5.  Select the required service team from the drop-down and click **Save.**
