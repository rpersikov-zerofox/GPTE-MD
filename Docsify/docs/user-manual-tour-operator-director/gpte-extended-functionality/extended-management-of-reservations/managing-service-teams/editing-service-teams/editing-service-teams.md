# Editing Service Teams

To modify service team information,

1.  On the Company profile menu click **Users**.

![image-20201019-193237.jpg](/assets/image-20201019-193237.jpg)

2.  Go to the **Service teams** tab.

![image-20201019-193342.png](/assets/image-20201019-193342.png)

3.  Open the required service team profile and click\*\* Edit\*\*.
4.  Make necessary corrections and click **Save**.
