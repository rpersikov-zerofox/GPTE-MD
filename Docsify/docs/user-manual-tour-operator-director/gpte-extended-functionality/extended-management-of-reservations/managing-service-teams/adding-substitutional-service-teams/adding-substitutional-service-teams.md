# Adding substitutional service teams

To add a substitutional service team,

1.  In the Substitutional team section, click **Add**. The Substitutional service team window appears:

![image-20200924-114558.jpg](/assets/image-20200924-114558.jpg)

2.  Select one or several teams to be substitute teams for the selected services or clients.
3.  Click **Add.** The selected team appear on the Substitutional teams list. To delete one or several teams, click the Delete icon in the corresponding line.
