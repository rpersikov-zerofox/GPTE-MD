# Configuring Service Team Notifications

You can configure sending notifications to the e-mail address specified in the service team profile.

![image-20200924-115056.png](/assets/image-20200924-115056.png)

For that,

1.  On the Company profile menu click **Users.**
2.  Go to the **Service team** tab.
3.  Open the required service team profile and go to the **Notification Settings** tab.
4.  Click **Edit**to switch to editing mode.
5.  Select the actions about which you want the service team to be notified about.
6.  Click **Save**.
