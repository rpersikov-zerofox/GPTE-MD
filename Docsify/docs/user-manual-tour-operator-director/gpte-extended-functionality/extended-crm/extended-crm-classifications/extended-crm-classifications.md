# Extended CRM Classifications

**Extended CRM Classifications** allow you to:

- Mark your clients as VIP / high / low / normal priority;
- Classify your clients depending on their industry and company size;
- Differentiate potential and actual clients.

![2024-11-18_15-28-13.jpg](/assets/2024-11-18_15-28-13.jpg)

To configure classification for a client,

1.  On the My Company menu, click on **Agencies/Distributors** or **Corporate clients**.
2.  Choose the client.
3.  In the field **Industry**of\*\* Information \*\*tab, specify the necessary one.
4.  In the field **Company** size , specify the number of employees.
5.  Click **Save**.
