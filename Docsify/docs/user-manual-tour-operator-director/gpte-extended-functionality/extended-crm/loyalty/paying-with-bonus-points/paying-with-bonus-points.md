# Paying with bonus points

Starting from the second booking a traveler can use the loyalty points for payment (assuming that all the necessary settings have been preconfigured in the system). On the payment stage the traveler will be asked whether to use the points right now or keep them and accumulate more for future bookings.

When you use loyalty points, they are automatically converted into the money equivalent according to the system settings. The amount to be paid is a total sum of booked services excluding money equivalent of the loyalty amount.
