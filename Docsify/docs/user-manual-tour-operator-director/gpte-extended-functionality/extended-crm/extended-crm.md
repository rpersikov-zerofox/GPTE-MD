# Extended CRM

**Extended CRM** is an ability to combine online booking engine with managing relationships with your clients within single platform. With Extended CRM set of modules you can:

- [Classify your clients](/wiki/spaces/GPTEUG/pages/916751734);
- [Track opportunities with potential clients](/wiki/spaces/GPTEUG/pages/916915270);
- [Manage marketing campaigns](/wiki/spaces/GPTEUG/pages/916915277);
- [Keep history of communications with clients](/wiki/spaces/GPTEUG/pages/922288808);
- [View overall analytics upon clients](/wiki/spaces/GPTEUG/pages/916686079).
