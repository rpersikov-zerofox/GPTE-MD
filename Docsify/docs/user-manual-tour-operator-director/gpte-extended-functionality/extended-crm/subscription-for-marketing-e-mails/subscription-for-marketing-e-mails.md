# Subscription for marketing e-mails

There's possibility to specify who from your clients should receive marketing e-mails. You can use this option to organize mass mails.

To activate marketing e-mails,

1.  On the Navigation menu, point to **Clients**, and click **Private** clients. The Private clients page appears.
2.  Choose the client. Click **Settings** and then **Edit**.
3.  Activate Receive marketing e-mails option.
4.  Click **Save**.
