# Extended CRM Opportunities

**This functionality is currently available via administration panel.**

**Extended CRM Opportunities** allow you to handle "big deals" with potential clients. Thus you can participate in tenders for corporate travel management or hold negotiations with large Agency chains and within the system reflect the current status of each such opportunity and assign who is responsible for it.

![image-20200922-164711.jpg](/assets/image-20200922-164711.jpg)

To configure an opportunity,

1.  On the Clients menu, click on **Agencies/Distributors**, **Corporate clients** or **Private**clients.
2.  Choose the client.
3.  Go to **Opportunities** tab and click **Create**.
4.  In the Opportunities menu specify all the necessary information.
5.  Click **Save**.
