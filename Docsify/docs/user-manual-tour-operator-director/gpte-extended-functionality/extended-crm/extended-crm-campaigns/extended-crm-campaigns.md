# Extended CRM Campaigns

**Extended CRM Campaigns** allow you to manage marketing campaigns. Thus, you can plan what steps towards gaining new clients you're going to undertake, what is the allocated budget and who is responsible for each campaign.

![2024-11-18_15-36-07.jpg](/assets/2024-11-18_15-36-07.jpg)

To configure a campaign,

1.  On the **My Company** menu, click **Campaigns**.
2.  In order to create a new campaign, click **Create**.
3.  Specify all the necessary information.
4.  Click **Save**.
