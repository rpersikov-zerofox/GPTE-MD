# Booking

To proceed with booking a complex tour,

1.  Fill in tourists data. Specify the following information: \* Full name; \* Date of birth; \* Nationality; \* Passport number and expiry date; \* Contact number and e-mail.

![image-20200924-112423.jpg](/assets/image-20200924-112423.jpg)

Additionally, you can leave a comment for a complex tour provider. Note however that leaving a comment can delay processing the reservation. 2. Select payment method:

![image-20200924-112507.jpg](/assets/image-20200924-112507.jpg)

3.  Read cancellation terms and conditions and select the respective check box:

![image-20200924-112536.jpg](/assets/image-20200924-112536.jpg)

4.  Click **Book** to proceed to complete the reservation.
5.  After you click book, the Confirmation window appears. Click **Complete reservation**in order to confirm the reservation.
6.  On the Reservation page, click **Voucher** to obtain a voucher.
7.  Issue or get an invoice for settlements with suppliers or/and clients. For more information, please see Reservations.
