# Choice of Services of Complex Tours

On this stage select the services to be included into the complex tour. In case you are satisfied with the services included into the tour, just click **Select** either opposite the complex tour title or at the bottom of the page:

![image-20200924-112206.jpg](/assets/image-20200924-112206.jpg)

**Replacing offers**

If you are not fully satisfied with the services included in the complex tour and want to view other offers,

1.  Click **view other alternatives** opposite the service you want to replace.

![image-20200924-112252.jpg](/assets/image-20200924-112252.jpg)

2.  On the offers page, choose the preferred offer and click\*\* Select \*\*opposite it:

![image-20200924-112325.jpg](/assets/image-20200924-112325.jpg)

3.  The original offer is replaces with the one you have chosen.
