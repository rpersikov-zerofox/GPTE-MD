# Booking of Dynamic package

On the final stage of booking,

1.  Fill the tourist data. Specify the following information: \* Full name; \* Date of birth; \* Citizenship; \* Passport number and issue & expire date; \* Contact phone number and e-mail.

![2024-11-18_11-17-54.jpg](/assets/2024-11-18_11-17-54.jpg)

1.  Click **Next step**.
2.  Select payment method.
3.  Read cancellation terms and conditions and select the respective check boxes.
4.  Click **Book** to finish the booking procedure.
5.  On the Reservation page, click **Voucher** to obtain a voucher.
6.  Issue or get an invoice for settlements with suppliers or/and clients. For more information, please see Reservations.
