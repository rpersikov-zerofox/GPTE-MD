# Choice of services Dynamic package

On the Search results page you can view the offers fitting your search criteria. If you are satisfied with the services included in the offer, click *Offer price* (ex. -\$1.34) button at the Dynamic package offer and then click **Book**:

![2024-11-18_11-08-14.jpg](/assets/2024-11-18_11-08-14.jpg)

**Selecting dynamic package alternatives**

In case you are not completely satisfied with the service included in the dynamic package,

1.  Click **View room offers**.
2.  Choose the preferred offer and click corresponding offer button:
3.  Click **Book**.
