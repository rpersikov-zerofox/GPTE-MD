# Invoice payment

While paying for issued invoice, it's possible to enter payment date manually.

It is necessary in case the payment bill outside the system was made in the past and you need to indicate the exact date of payment.

To amend the payment date click to the calendar icon and choose desired date.

To set up time of payment click on time box and change it manually. Then click "ok".

![2024-12-24_12-31-27.jpg](/assets/2024-12-24_12-31-27.jpg)

Previously it was possible to cancel transactions (invoices) in the Payment Schedule even if the payment is made already which could cause issues when the transaction is cancelled by mistake and lead to the additional investigation of the initial data to restore the transaction. The same situation could take place with backdating transactions when pay date can be edited to be in past. To avoid such situations the ability to cancel paid transactions or backdate transactions before a certain date was added to a role 'Director' only.
