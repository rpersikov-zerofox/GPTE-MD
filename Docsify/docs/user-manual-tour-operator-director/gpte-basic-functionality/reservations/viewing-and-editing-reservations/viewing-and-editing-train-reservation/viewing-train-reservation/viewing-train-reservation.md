# Viewing Train Reservation

**This functionality is currently available via administration panel.**

The reservation contains all information on the train that you have booked.

![image-20201012-125747.jpg](/assets/image-20201012-125747.jpg)

To view the newly-created reservation,

1.  On the Reservations menu, click **View reservations** and select the required reservation. On the Services tab you will see the following information:

- **Train information**: in this field you can see the train name and the train number.
- **Departure date**: the time when the train departures.
- **Arrival date:**the time when the train arrives.
- **Duration**: the time that the travel lasts.
- Coach class:
- Service class:
- Coach number:
- Seat (s) number (s):
- **Supplier**: the name of a supplier (GDS) of the given train.
- **Additional services:**in this field you can see extra services included in this reservation.
- **Guests:** in this field you can see names and family names of tourists booked on this hotel, their nationality, contact data, email.
- **Custom fields:** in this field is it configured the following information of a separate corporate client: Information, Values, Conditions. For more information see [Custom Fields.](https://gp-team.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPTEUG/pages/922616190)
- **Comments**: additional information about this reservation.
- **Status**: the current status of the order.
- **Price**: up-to-date price of the booked hotel for the tourists specified in the order.
- **Supplier's price**: supplier\'s price of the hotel.
- **Client's price**: price at which the service was sold to a travel agency. The figure in brackets shows the amount that the agency is to return to a tour operator.
- **Penalty**: information on penalties provided for each hotel according to the rules of the current tariff.
