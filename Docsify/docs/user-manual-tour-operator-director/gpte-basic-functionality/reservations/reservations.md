# Reservations

GP Travel Enterprise stores all reservations in a centralized directory for easy access and management. The Reservations module serves as your command center for managing bookings, tracking customer orders, and coordinating travel arrangements.

## Access Reservations

To view the reservations list, click **Reservations** in the navigation bar:

![Reservations navigation](/assets/2024-10-29_22-47-45.png)

## Reservation Management

The Reservations page allows you to:

- **Search** - Find specific reservations using filters and search criteria
- **View and Edit** - Access reservation details and make modifications
- **Assign Managers** - Designate or reassign reservation managers
- **Process Invoices** - Issue customer invoices and receive supplier invoices
- **Add Documents** - Attach relevant files and documentation
- **Import PNR Data** - Import passenger name records from external systems

## Reservation Workflow

A typical reservation follows this workflow:

1. **Create** - Enter booking details and customer information
2. **Confirm** - Verify availability and confirm with suppliers
3. **Document** - Add required documents and vouchers
4. **Invoice** - Generate and send customer invoices
5. **Manage** - Track payment, modifications, and cancellations
6. **Complete** - Finalize the reservation after travel completion

**Note:** The specific workflow may vary based on your company's business processes and the type of reservation (hotel, flight, tour, etc.).
