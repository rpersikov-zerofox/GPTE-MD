# Price-Lists setting for excursions

To set up a price-list for excursion,

On **Products** → Excursion **Prices** tab → **Price-lists** sub-tab click **Create**, *Price-list creating* page appears:

![2024-11-12_11-04-17.jpg](/assets/2024-11-12_11-04-17.jpg)

1.  On the Price-list page, specify the name of the price-list, specify the applied tariff, the days of the week on which it will be valid and the price-list description. If needed, you can create different price-lists for week days and weekends.
2.  In the Prices section, select the method of price calculation (considering the service and tourist category) and specify the rates for all trips and categories of tourists:

- In case **the price is the same for all the services** is selected, one single price rate is set for the service irrespective of the excursion service or the schedule.
- In case **the price depends on the type and the time of the service** is selected, you should specify a separate price rate for the types of excursion taking their schedule into consideration too.

1.  Click **Save**.

The prices for activities sometimes depend on quantity of passengers in the group, and such logic will also be supported in GP Travel Enterprise. For each product you can create as many ranges as required according to your original prices from suppliers.
