# Deactivating Insurances

In order to deactivate an insurance,

1.  On the **Products** menu, click **Insurance**.
2.  Open the required insurance. The Insurance page appears.

![2024-11-13_17-30-58.jpg](/assets/2024-11-13_17-30-58.jpg)

3.  Switch the **Active** toggle off. The insurance will be moved to archive.
4.  Click **Save**.

The deactivated insurances are not deleted permanently. They are moved to archive and can be restored. To view the list of archived insurances, in the table of self-operated insurances, switch **Active**toggle off:

![image-20201013-100309.png](/assets/image-20201013-100309.png)

To restore the archived insurances, repeat Step 1 and Step 2, then switch **Active**toggle on and click **Save**.
