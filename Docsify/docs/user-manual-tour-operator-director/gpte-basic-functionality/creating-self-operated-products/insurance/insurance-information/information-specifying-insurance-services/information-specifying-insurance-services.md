# Information: Specifying Insurance Services

For the insurance, you should specify its policy type as well as its basic terms: franchise and coverage.

To do so,

1.  Open the required insurance.
2.  On the Information tab, **Coverage options** sub-tab, click **Create**. *Create insurance service type* page appears:

![2024-11-13_16-05-52.jpg](/assets/2024-11-13_16-05-52.jpg)

3.  On the *Create insurance service type* page, specify the following information: 1. **Name:** type the name of an insurance service. 2. **Currency**: specify the currency of an insurance service. 3. **Franchise:** in the box enter the sum of the franchise. 4. **Coverage:** in the box enter the coverage sum. 5. **Description:** describe the insurance service.
4.  Click **Save**. The insurance service is added to the director of insurance services.
