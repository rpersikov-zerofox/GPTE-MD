# Information: Specifying General Information

To create a new self-operated insurance service,

1.  On the **Products** menu click **Insurance**.
2.  On the Insurance page, click **Create**. *Create new policy type* page appears:

![2024-11-13_15-59-55.jpg](/assets/2024-11-13_15-59-55.jpg)

3.  On the *Create new policy type* page, specify the following information 1. **Name:** type the name of the insurance. 2. **Supplier:** select the insurance service supplier. 3. **Default currency:** select the currency to use for settlements with suppliers. The currency will also be used for setting tariffs, price-lists, etc. 4. **Countries covered by insurance:** specify the set of countries covered by the insurance. 1. In case the coverage is not restricted to a certain set of countries, click **All countries**. 2. To restrict the list of countries covered by the insurance, click **Select** **from the list**, then click **Add**. The list of countries appears. Select the countries of your choice and click Save. 5. **Insurance is available for citizens of the following countries:** in the group, specify the citizens of what countries can apply for the insurance. Follow the steps described above. 6. **Insurance risks:** select the check boxes to indicate which accidents are covered by the insurance. 7. **Documents - Insurance regulations:** Upload a file highlighting insurance documents, regulations or explaining any other information on the service. 1. To upload a file from your PC, in the **Upload file** group, click **Add**, browse to the required file, click **Open** and then click **Upload**.

After you finished to fill in all the details click **Save**. Your policy type will be saved into the system. Open the created insurance product and navigate to **Description** tab and enter corresponding information:

- **Short description**: type the insurance service description. The description will be displayed on the Search results page.
- **Fill description**: type the description of the service. This description will be displayed in the window with the detailed information on the insurance.
- **Important information**: type additional important details.

![2024-11-13_16-03-55.jpg](/assets/2024-11-13_16-03-55.jpg)

After you finished to fill in all the descriptions click **Save**.
