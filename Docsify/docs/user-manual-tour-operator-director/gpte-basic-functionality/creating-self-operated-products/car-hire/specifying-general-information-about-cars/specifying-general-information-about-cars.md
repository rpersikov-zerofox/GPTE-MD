# Specifying General Information about Cars

To add information about a new car,

1.  In the **Products** menu, click **Cars Hire**tab.
2.  On the Cars page, click **Create**. Create new car hire page appears.

![2024-11-11_22-04-44.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_22-04-44.jpg)

1.  On the New car hire page, specify the following information: 1. **Car name:** enter the name of a new car. 2. **Product name for supplier**: add a car name as a supplier calls it.\*\* **The product name for the supplier is used in notifications to the supplier. 3.**Supplier:\*\* select a supplier that offers a product. 4. **Default currency:** specify the currency for settlements with suppliers. The currency that you select will be also used for setting price-lists, discounts, etc. 5. **Offices:** select rental offices where a car is available. For more information on rental offices, please click Managing rental offices for self-operated car products. 6. **Key facts**: specify category, transmission type, class, fuel type, number of doors, number of suitcases, number of passengers. Check relevant Features checkboxes. 7. **Internal notes**: enter additional information about the car in the notes.
2.  Click **Save**.
3.  On the **Description** tab of the created car hire product specify: 1. **Short description:** add general information about the car. This description will be displayed on the search results page. 2. \*\* Full description:\*\* add detailed information about the car. This description will be displayed in the window with the detailed description of the car hire product. 3. **Important information**: add additional important information about the car hire product.
4.  Click **Save**.
5.  On the **Photo** tab add a photo of the vehicle. The picture that you add in this section is displayed in the car rent search results. 1. To upload a picture from your PC, click the\*\* Select photos\*\* field, browse to the required picture, click **Open**, and then click **Upload**.

**Managing Rental Offices**

If you have several rental offices, you can restrict the availability of cars at some of the offices.

To create a rental office,

1.  On the Suppliers menu, click **Suppliers.**
2.  Open the profile of the required supplier - the supplier who provides the car hire service and who is selected in the car hire product contract. For example, assume the car service is provided by the **HERTZ** supplier. On the Suppliers page, search for the **HERTZ** supplier and click it. The Supplier page appears.
3.  On the Supplier page, go to the **Offices** tab.
4.  On the Offices tab, click **Create**. The Create new office window appears.

![2024-11-11_22-21-21.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_22-21-21.jpg)

1.  In the window, fill in the required information. The fields to be filled in mandatory are marked with an asterisk (*). Among other please pay attention to the following fields: 1.Type:*\* from the drop-down select either place in the city or airport. Depending on the selected item, the subsequent fields will be either Street and Postal code, Airport or Accomodation. 2. **Map:** after you enter the office location or select an airport from the dropdown, click Map to see the location on the map. The address date are automatically processed and the location on the map is shown. 3. **Latitude, Longitude:** to see the location on the map or to correct the location, you can type either the office address or coordinates. Locate mouse pointer into the Latitude and Longitude boxes and enter the office location coordinates.
2.  Click **Save**. The created office is displayed in the list of supplier\'s offices

Let us assume that the car service supplier posses the following offices:

It is the offices depicted in the figure above that are available for selection in the car hire contract.

**To specify the offices where the car hire product is available,**

1.  Open the profile of the required car hire product.
2.  On the **Information** tab,\*\* Info\*\* sub-tab, operate in the **Car can be picked up at the following rental offices** section:

![2024-11-11_22-26-35.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_22-26-35.jpg)

3.  To make a car hire product available at all the supplier offices, click **all**.
4.  To restrict the set of office where a car hire product is available, click **selected only**. The additional **Add** button appears.
5.  Click **Add**. The Select car rental office window appears:

![Screenshot 2023-09-08 at 09.26.09.jpg](/assets/Screenshot-2023-09-08-at-09.26.09.jpg)

6.  In the window, select the offices of your choice and click **Save**. The selected rental offices are added to the car hire contract.
7.  Click **Save**.
