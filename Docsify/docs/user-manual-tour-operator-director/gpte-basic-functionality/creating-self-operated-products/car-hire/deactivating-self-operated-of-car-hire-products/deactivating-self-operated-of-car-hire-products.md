# Deactivating Self-operated of Car Hire Products

In order to deactivate a self-operated car,

1.  On the **Products** menu, click **Car Hire**.
2.  Open the profile of the required car hire product. The Car rent page appears:

![2024-11-11_23-23-14.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_23-23-14.jpg)

1.  On the Car rent page, switch the **Active** toggle off. The car will be moved to archive.
2.  Click **Save**.

The deactivated cars are not deleted permanently. They are moved to archive and can be restored. To view the list of archived cars, in the table of self-operated cars, switch **Active**toggle off:

![2024-11-11_23-24-31.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_23-24-31.jpg)

To restore the archived cars, repeat **Step 1** and **Step 2**, then switch the **Inactive** toggle on.
