# Viewing/Editing Self-operated of Car Hire Products

To view or edit a car hire product,

1.  On the **Products** menu, click **Car Hire**.
2.  In the table of cars, click the required one.
3.  The Car rent page appears:

![2024-11-11_22-17-34.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_22-17-34.jpg)

4.  Go to the required tab and make necessary corrections.
5.  Click **Save**.
