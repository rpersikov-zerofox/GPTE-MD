# Viewing List of Car Hire Products

To view the list of car hire products, on the **Products** menu, click **Car Hire**. All the previously created cars are arranged in the table:

![2024-11-11_23-17-54.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_23-17-54.jpg)

- The entries in the table are sorted by the Name. In order to change the sorting criterion, click the corresponding column header.
- To change the order of sorting, click the column one more time. \* For example in case sorting by name is applied, the entries in the table are sorted from A to Z. To apply the reverse sorting order, click Name column head. The entries are now sorted from Z to A. \*
- In case the number of entries in the list is sufficient or if you need to find a particular charter, use the search form at the top of the page:

![2024-11-11_23-18-49.png](/assets/2024-11-11_23-18-49.png)

- In the search form, fill in the boxes and click **Filter**. The list of car hire products will be contracted to those fitting your search criteria. To clear the search form and return to the initial state, click **Reset**.
