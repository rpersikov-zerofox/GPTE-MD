# Creating Additional Services of Car Hire Products

You can specify the list of additional tourists available for car hire products. To add an additional service,

1.  On the **Products** menu, click **Cars Hire**.
2.  Click the required car hire product.
3.  On the Information tab, **Extra services sub-tab**, click **Create**. The New Extra Services window appears:

![2024-11-11_22-32-38.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_22-32-38.jpg)

4.  In the New extra services window, specify the following information: 1. **Name:** enter the name of the additional service. 2. **Category:** select the category of the additional service (chauffeur, insurance, etc.) In case there is no item that suites you, select **Other**. 3. **Calculation type:** In the group, by default the method of price calculation depending on the number of days the service. 4. **Offer this extra service:** In the group, specify the conditions of the additional service availability. 1. In case you want the service to be available irrespective of the pick-up and drop-off locations, click **always**. 2. Alternatively, if you want to offer the additional service only if the pick-up and drop-off locations are different, click the respective option. 3. If you want to specify the exact pick-up and drop-off locations, click only **with the following pick-up and drop-off locations**. The additional form appears:

![2024-11-11_22-34-53.png](/assets/2024-11-11_22-34-53.png)

![2024-11-11_22-35-39.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_22-35-39.jpg)

5.  Click **Save**. The additional service is added to the list of available additional services and displayed on the Additional services page.
