# Deactivating Cruises

In order to deactivate a cruise,

1.  On the **Products** menu, click **Cruise**.
2.  Open the required Cruise.
3.  The Cruise page appears.
4.  Switch the Active toggle off. The cruise will be moved to archive.

![2024-11-14_16-05-31.png](/assets/2024-11-14_16-05-31.png)

5.  Click **Save**.
6.  The deactivated cruises are not deleted permanently. They are moved to archive and can be restored. To view the list of archived cruises, in the table of self-operated cruises, switch **Active**toggle off:

![2024-11-14_16-06-52.jpg](/assets/2024-11-14_16-06-52.jpg)

To restore the archived cruises repeat **Step 1** and **Step 2**, then switch the **Inactive** toggle on.
