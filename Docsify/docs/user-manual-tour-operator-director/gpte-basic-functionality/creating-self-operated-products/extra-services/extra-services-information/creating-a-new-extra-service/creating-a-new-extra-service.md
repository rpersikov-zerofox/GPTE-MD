# Creating a New Extra Service

To add information about a new extra service,

1.  On the Products menu, click **Extras.**
2.  On the **Extra Services** page, click **Create**. *Create New Extra Service* page appears:

![2024-11-13_15-09-35.jpg](/assets/2024-11-13_15-09-35.jpg)

1.  *On Create New Extra Service* page specify the following information:

- **Name**: enter the extra service name.
- **Product name for Supplier**: add a product name as a supplier calls it.\*\* \*\*The product name for the supplier is used in notifications to the supplier.
- **Supplier**: select the extra service service supplier.
- **Key facts**: select the extra service type - either service or tourist.
- **Default currency**: from the drop-down, select the currency to use for settlements with service supplier. The selected currency will also be used for configuring tariffs, price-lists, etc.
- **Countries/Cities where the service is provided**: specify the location(s) where extra services are offered.

After you have finished to fill in all the details click **Save**. Your extra service will be saved into the system. Open the created extra service product and navigate to **Description** tab and enter corresponding information:

![2024-11-13_15-30-41.jpg](/assets/2024-11-13_15-30-41.jpg)

- **Short description**: briefly describe the service. This description will be displayed on the search results page.
- **Description**: enter the service description. This description will be displayed in the window with the detailed description of the service.
- **Important information**: enter additional important details.

1.  Click **Save**.

To upload a picture for the extra service from your PC, navigate to **Photo** tab:

![2024-11-13_15-32-39.jpg](/assets/2024-11-13_15-32-39.jpg)

- click at **Select photos**field, browse to the required picture, click **Open** and upload a picture;
- click **Save**.
