# Creating Services Options

To specify the general parameters of the new extra service and create the constituent services,

1.  Open the profile of the required self-operated extra service.
2.  On the Information tab, **Services** sub-tab click **Create**. Service page appears:

![2024-11-13_15-16-04.jpg](/assets/2024-11-13_15-16-04.jpg)

1.  On the Service page, specify the following information:

- **Name**: enter the name of the service.
- **Description**: enter the service description.

1.  Click **Save**. The service is added to the list of extra services.
