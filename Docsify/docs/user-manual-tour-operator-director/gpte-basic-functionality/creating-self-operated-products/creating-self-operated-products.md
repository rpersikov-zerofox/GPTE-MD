# Creating Self-Operated Products

GP Travel Enterprise provides comprehensive tools for creating and managing self-operated travel products—services you own and operate directly. Use the GP Inventory module to build your product catalog, set pricing, and manage availability for hotels, tours, transfers, excursions, and other travel services.

## What Are Self-Operated Products?

Self-operated products are travel services that you:

- Own and operate yourself (such as your own tours or transfers)
- Have direct contracts with suppliers (hotels without online connectivity)
- Manage pricing and availability independently

These products appear in search results alongside third-party supplier offerings, giving customers access to your complete inventory.

## GP Inventory Module

The Inventory module provides three core capabilities:

### 1. Product Descriptions

Create detailed product information:

- Short and detailed descriptions
- Photo galleries
- Service specifications
- Amenities and features

### 2. Pricing Configuration

Set up flexible pricing structures:

- Seasonal price lists
- Discount rules and promotions
- Cancellation policies and penalties
- Multiple tariff options

### 3. Availability Management

Control inventory and bookings:

- Room allotments for hotels and accommodations
- Seat availability for tours and transfers
- Schedule and timetable management
- On-request booking configuration

## Product Creation Workflow

### Step 1: General Product Description

Enter basic product information:

1. **Basic Details**
   - Product name and type
   - Supplier selection (your company or contracted supplier)
   - Transaction currency
   - Product description

2. **Service Categories**
   - Accommodation categories (hotel star ratings)
   - Service classes (economy, business, luxury)
   - Product types and classifications

3. **Traveler Categories**
   - Adult (created by default)
   - Child (with age ranges and special pricing)
   - Infant or senior (if applicable)

4. **Service Schedule**
   - Operating hours
   - Service start times
   - Seasonal availability

5. **Additional Services**
   - Optional add-ons
   - Extra amenities
   - Supplementary products

### Step 2: Price Configuration

Set up pricing structure:

1. **Create Tariffs**
   - Define pricing calculation rules
   - Set marketing terms and conditions

2. **Build Price Lists**
   - Main product pricing
   - Included services pricing
   - Date-based pricing variations

3. **Additional Service Pricing**
   - Price lists for optional extras
   - Supplement charges

4. **Configure Discounts**
   - Discount types (early booking, last minute, etc.)
   - Application terms and conditions
   - Discount priority and stacking rules

5. **Set Penalties**
   - Cancellation fees
   - Modification charges
   - No-show penalties

### Step 3: Availability Setup

Define product availability:

1. **Allotment Configuration**
   - Set room or seat allotments (if contracted with suppliers)
   - Define release periods

2. **On-Request Booking**
   - Configure manual confirmation workflow
   - Set confirmation timelines

3. **Blackout Dates**
   - Specify non-available dates
   - Manage seasonal closures

## Prerequisites

Before creating self-operated products:

1. **Supplier Setup Required**
   - Create your company as a supplier (for products you operate)
   - Add external suppliers (for direct contract products)
   - See [Managing Suppliers](../managing-suppliers-purchase-settings/managing-suppliers/managing-suppliers.md)

2. **Minimum Configuration for Sales**
   - At least one tariff created
   - Availability configured for desired date range
   - Pricing defined for applicable dates

**Important:** Products only appear in search results when they have both pricing and availability configured.

## Publishing Products

After creating a product:

1. **Review Configuration** - Verify all details are correct
2. **Set Availability** - Configure available dates and inventory
3. **Create Tariff** - Define at least one pricing tariff
4. **Test Search** - Verify product appears in search results
5. **Monitor Bookings** - Track reservations and inventory

## Product Management

Once created, you can:

- View and edit product details
- Modify pricing and availability
- Deactivate products temporarily
- Update descriptions and images
- Monitor booking performance
