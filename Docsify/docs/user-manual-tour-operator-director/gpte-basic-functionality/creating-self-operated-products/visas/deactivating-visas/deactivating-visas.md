# Deactivating Visas

In order to deactivate a visa,

1.  On the **Products** menu click **Visa**.
2.  Open the required visa. The Visa page appears.

![2024-11-13_15-02-21.jpg](/assets/2024-11-13_15-02-21.jpg)

3.  Switch the **Active** toggle off. The visa will be moved to archive.

![2024-11-13_15-02-21.jpg](/assets/2024-11-13_15-02-21.jpg)

4.  Click **Save**.

Alternatively, visas can be deactivated in the Visas list:

![2024-11-13_15-03-48.png](/assets/2024-11-13_15-03-48.png)

The deactivated visas are not deleted permanently. They are moved to archive and can be restored. To view the list of archived visas, in the table of self-operated visas, switch **Active**toggle off:

![image-20201012-150349.jpg](/assets/image-20201012-150349.jpg)

To restore the archived visas, repeat Step 1 and Step 2, then switch the **Active** toggle on and click **Save**.
