# Setting Duration of Stay

Specify the duration of stay, that is the number of days for which visa is issued,

1.  Open the profile of the required visa.
2.  On the Information tab → **Duration** sub-tab, click **Create**. *Create duration of stay* page appears:

![2024-11-13_13-41-41.jpg](/assets/2024-11-13_13-41-41.jpg)

3.  On the *Create duration of stay* page, specify the following information: 1. **Name:** enter the name of the period of duration into the box. 2. **Default:** select the check box in order to apply the duration of stay as default. 3. **Description:** describe the duration of stay.
4.  Click **Save**. The new duration period is saved and stored in the common directory of visa periods of duration.
