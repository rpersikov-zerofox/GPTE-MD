# Creating Extra Services for Visas

You can specify Extra services that are offered for tourists. For instance, you can offer an insurance together with the visa services.

To add an Extra service to a contract of a self-operated visa,

1.  Open the required self-operated visa.
2.  On the Information tab, click **Extra services**. *Create extra service* page appears:

![2024-11-13_13-46-47.jpg](/assets/2024-11-13_13-46-47.jpg)

3.  On the *Create extra service* page, specify the following information: 1. **Name:** type the name of the Extra service. 2. **Price per:** In the group, specify the method of price calculation: either per service or per tourist. In case you select per service, while creating price-lists for Extra services, you will be asked to specify the price for the Extra service itself irrespective of the number of tourists who books the service. In case you select per tourist, the total price for the Extra service is the sum of the prices payed by the tourists who buy the service. 3. **Offer this extra service:** In the group, specify the conditions of the Extra service availability. 1. In case you want the Extra service to be available for all services, click **always**. 2. Alternatively, in case you want to restrict the Extra service availability, click **only when booking the following services**. 1. In the appeared section, select the required visa types. The Extra service will be offered for tourists only in case a tourist books the selected visa type.

![2024-11-13_13-48-40.png](/assets/2024-11-13_13-48-40.png)

d.\*\* Description:\*\* enter the Extra service description. This description will be displayed on the search results page and in the window with the detailed description of the service.

1.  Click **Save**. The Extra service is added to the list of available Extra services and displayed on the Extra services page.
