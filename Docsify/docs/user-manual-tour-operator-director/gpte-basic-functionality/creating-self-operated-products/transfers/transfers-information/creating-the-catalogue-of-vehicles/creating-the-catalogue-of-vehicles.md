# Creating the Catalogue of Vehicles

The next step of configuring transfers is to specify transfer categories: here you can differentiate your transfer services by categories or by the type of vehicle (e.g. standard car, luxury car, coach or bus). To create a new transport category,

1.  Open the required transfer.
2.  On the Information tab, in the **Catalogue** sub-tab, click **Create**. **Create category** window appears:

![2024-11-11_15-04-00.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_15-04-00.jpg)

3.  Enter the name of the transport category.
4.  Click **Save**. The new category is created and is available for selection while setting transfer schedule.
