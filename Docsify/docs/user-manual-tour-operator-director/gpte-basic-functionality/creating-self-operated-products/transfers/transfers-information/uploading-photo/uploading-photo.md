# Uploading photo

You can demonstrate the transfer car by showing it with the images. To add the images to the description of your transfer, do the following.

## **Uploading images from your PC**

To upload an image from your PC,

1.  On the **Photo** tab, click at Select photos field.

![2024-11-11_15-00-33.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_15-00-33.jpg)

2.  Browse to the image you want to upload, and click **Open**.
3.  Click **Upload**.
4.  Select the respective image category from the drop-down list below the image.
5.  Click **Save**.
