# Deactivating Transfers

In order to deactivate a transfer,

1.  On the **Products** menu, click **Transfers**.
2.  Open the profile of the required transfer. The transfer page appears:

![2024-11-11_16-37-23.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_16-37-23.jpg)

3.  On the Transfer page, switch the **Active**toggle off. The transfer will be moved to archive.

![2024-11-11_16-39-42.png](/assets/2024-11-11_16-39-42.png)

4.  Click **Save**.

The deactivated transfers are not deleted permanently. They are moved to archive and can be restored. To view the list of archived transfers, in the table of self-operated transfers, switch the **Active**toggle off:

![2024-11-11_16-40-38.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_16-40-38.jpg)

To restore the archived transfers, repeat **Step 1** and **Step 2**, then switch the **Active** toggle on and click **Save**.
