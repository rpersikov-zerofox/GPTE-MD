# Diactivating Charters

**In order to deactivate a charter**,

1.  Open the required charter page.
2.  Switch the Active toggle off. The charter will be moved to archive.

![2024-11-11_13-39-58.png](/assets/2024-11-11_13-39-58.png)

1.  Click Save.

The deactivated charters are not deleted permanently. They are moved to archive and can be restored. **To view archived charters**, in the table of self-operated charters, switch Active toggle off:

![2024-11-11_13-40-55.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_13-40-55.jpg)

To restore the archived charters:

1.  Open the required charter page.
2.  Swith the Active toggle on
3.  Click Save.
