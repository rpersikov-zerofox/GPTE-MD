# Deactivating Tours

In order to deactivate a tour,

1.  On the **Products** menu, click **Tours**.
2.  Open the required tour. The Tour page appears.

![2024-11-13_13-11-57.jpg](/assets/2024-11-13_13-11-57.jpg)

3.  Switch the **Active** toggle off. The tour will be moved to archive.

![2024-11-13_13-13-56.png](/assets/2024-11-13_13-13-56.png)

4.  Click **Save**.

The deactivated tours are not deleted permanently. They are moved to archive and can be restored. To view the list of archived tours, in the table of self-operated tours, switch **Active**toggle off:

![2024-11-13_13-14-54.png](/assets/2024-11-13_13-14-54.png)

To restore the archived tours repeat **Step 1** and **Step 2**, then switch the **Active** toggle on and click **Save**.
