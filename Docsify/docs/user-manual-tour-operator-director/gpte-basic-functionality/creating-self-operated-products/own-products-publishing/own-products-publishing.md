# Own Products Publishing

Preparation of own products for sales may take some time, so now there is a possibility to fully control this process - instead of immediate ability to see created products during search&book, now you can decide whether they are "Draft", "On approval" or "Published".

When the product is fully entered - it is Published to become available or sent On approval, if according to internal processes of the company some supervisor should approve products before publishing. Only "Published" products will become available for sales.

![2024-12-20_15-47-27.jpg](/assets/2024-12-20_15-47-27.jpg)

Stages are added for ALL products - Hotels, Transfers, Activities, etc.

By default it is set to "Draft".

By clicking on the dropdown, a list of necessary statuses appears, that includes:

- Draft
- Approval
- Published

Products in statuses "Draft" and "Approval" are not shown during search&book.

Products in statuses "Draft" and "Approval" are not shown in Suppliers Extranet.
