# Deactivating Accommodations

In order to deactivate accommodation,

1.  Open the Information tab of a required accommodation.
2.  Switch the Active toggle off. The accommodation will be moved to archive.

![2024-11-11_18-50-48.jpg](/assets/2024-11-11_18-50-48.jpg)

3.  Click **Save.**

The deactivated hotels are not deleted permanently. They are moved to archive and can be restored. To view archived entries, in the table of self-operated accommodation, switch **Active**toggle off:

![2024-11-11_18-51-50.png](/assets/2024-11-11_18-51-50.png)

To restore the archived accommodation, repeat Step 1 and Step 2, then switch the **Active** toggle on and click **Save**.
