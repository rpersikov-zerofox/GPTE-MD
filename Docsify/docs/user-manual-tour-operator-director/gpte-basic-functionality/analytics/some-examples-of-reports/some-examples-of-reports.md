# Some examples of reports

## Analytic Reports: Turnovers by suppliers

In this case the table shows sales results by suppliers sorted by the quantity of reservations for the requested period. Also in the table you see the summary of net coasts and turnovers as well as your income and average booking price per supplier.

Furthermore the system presents this information in the graphical form. That compares the turnovers among all your suppliers.

## Inventory Reports

1.  If you work with selfoperated hotels you might be interested in getting summary of prices in printable form. *During generation of this report you can choose -- either to get NET prices from suppliers or get SALES prices for some of your clients/partners (if you're going to send price list to him and it should already have his markup).*

![image-20200920-085951.png](/assets/image-20200920-085951.png)

2.  In order to see general statistic of availability of your selfoperated hotels, you may generate a report, which will show you this information in details. You should specify the period of time and download a report in Excel format.
