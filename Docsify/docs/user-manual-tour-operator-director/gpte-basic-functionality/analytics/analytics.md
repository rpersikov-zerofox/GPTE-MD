# Analytics

The Analytics module provides comprehensive reporting and analysis tools to monitor your business performance. Use these reports to track sales, evaluate partner relationships, analyze profitability, and make data-driven decisions.

## Overview

The Analytics module helps you:

- Monitor sales data across any time period
- Track reservations placed through agencies and purchased from suppliers
- Evaluate partner performance and profitability
- Identify trends and patterns in your business
- Make informed decisions about future priorities

## Report Categories

The system organizes reports into six categories:

### 1. Analytic Reports

Business activity and sales performance reports with detailed breakdowns:

- **Turnovers by**: Products, countries, cities, users, suppliers, agencies/distributors, corporate clients, sales channels, websites
- **Sales Dynamics**: Track sales trends over time
- **Email Reports**: Monitor email communications
- **User Login Reports**: Track system usage
- **Look-to-Book Ratio by**: Products, countries, cities, corporate clients, agencies/distributors
- **Summary Reports**: Accommodation overview and statistics

### 2. Corporate Reports

Corporate travel policy compliance monitoring:

- **Corporate Policy Violations**: List of policy breach incidents

### 3. Financial Reports

Detailed financial transaction records:

- **Customer Invoices**: Client billing records
- **Supplier Invoices**: Vendor payment tracking
- **Agency Sales**: Distribution partner financial data
- **Flight Sales**: Aviation-specific financial records

### 4. Inventory Reports

Pricing summaries and availability statistics for self-operated properties:

- **Hotels Availability Report**: Real-time availability status
- **Hotels Tariffs Report**: Current pricing information
- **Price Lists**: Comprehensive hotel pricing
- **Channel Manager Report**: Distribution channel data

### 5. Rooming Lists

Traveler manifests for check-ins, charters, and transfers:

Available for: Hotels, Tours, Complex Tours/Dynamic Packages, Visas, Events, Car Rentals, Transfers, Activities, Flights, Trains

### 6. Statement of Account

Financial summaries for clients and suppliers:

- **Client Statements**: Orders, invoices, payments, and account balances
- **Supplier Statements**: Vendor financial records and payment status

## Report Formats

All reports can be generated in two formats:

- **PDF** - For printing and formal documentation
- **Excel** - For further analysis and data manipulation

## Generate a Report

To create a new report:

1. In the navigation bar, click **Reports**.

   ![Reports menu](/assets/2024-10-29_16-30-13.jpg)

2. Select the report type you want to generate.

   ![Report selection](/assets/2024-10-29_16-30-23.png)

3. In the report configuration window:
   - Select the time period
   - Set the start and end dates
   - Choose the report format (PDF or Excel)

4. Click **PDF Report** to generate a PDF document, or **Excel Report** to generate a spreadsheet.

   The system compiles the requested data and displays your report.
