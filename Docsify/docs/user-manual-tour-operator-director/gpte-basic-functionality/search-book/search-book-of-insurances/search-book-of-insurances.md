# Search & Book of Insurances

Searh&Book of Insurances consists of 4 steps:

## Search

1.  On the Home page, in the search form, click the **Insurance** tab to expand the search form. The Insurance search form appears:

![2024-11-05_14-41-43.jpg](/assets/2024-11-05_14-41-43.jpg)

1.  On the search form, there are basic and additional search options. First, fill in the basic search options, which include: 1. **Destination** - select for which country you search insurance. 2. **Insurance period** - the time frame during which an insurance policy is effective. 3. **Days** - a number of days included into an insurance policy. 4. **Tourists** - a number of tourists whose interests are protected by an insurance policy.
2.  After the basic search options are specified you can click **Search** to initiate search process. Alternatively if you want to get more accurate results, click **Extra options** to expand the additional search parameters. They include: 1. Specify suppliers type(s) to be considered while searching flights offers selecting **Internal suppliers** and/or **External suppliers**check boxes\*\*Supplier:\*\* select preferred service suppler. 2. **Citizenship:**select the tourist citizenship. 3. Select\*\* Show commission \*\*check box to display agency commission values at prices.
3.  Click **Search** to initiate search process.

## Results

**Filter of results** In case you want to see more precise results and leave off unnecessary offers, use the **Filter form**on the left part of the screen:

![2024-11-06_10-30-50.jpg](/assets/2024-11-06_10-30-50.jpg)

Specify the following filtering parameters:

- **Price:** move the sliders across the scale or indicate the required range manually to limit the permissible price range.
- **Supplier:** select the required supplier.
- **Insurance name**: enter the required insurance name.
- **Insurance description**: enter the required insurance description.

Search results will be automatically updated according to the filters you applied.

**Search results** On the search results page all the offers meeting your search criteria are displayed. To continue the booking process, choose a required offer and click **Book**.

![2024-11-06_10-37-33.png](/assets/2024-11-06_10-37-33.png)

Use the following prompts:

1.  To sort search results, select the sorting parameter from the drop-down.

![2024-11-06_10-41-04.jpg](/assets/2024-11-06_10-41-04.jpg)

1.  Click **Detailed Info** to view all the available offer details.

![2024-11-06_10-41-57.png](/assets/2024-11-06_10-41-57.png)

1.  Choose the required offer and click **Book**.

## Booking

**Tourists** In the Tourists section, fill in tourist data, which include:

1.  Full name: First Name and Last Name.
2.  Date of birth.
3.  Citizenship.
4.  Contacts: E-mail and Phone number.
5.  Passport number.
6.  Address.

![2024-11-06_10-42-44.jpg](/assets/2024-11-06_10-42-44.jpg)

Add a comment to the supplier in the Comment field, if neede, and click **Next step**.

**Confirmation** Specify the payment method selecting a preferred one from the available options.

Select the terms of use check boxes and click\*\* Book\*\*:

![2024-11-06_10-47-05.jpg](/assets/2024-11-06_10-47-05.jpg)

## Voucher

After you click Book on Reservation confirmation page, a Reservation page opens with a pop-up confirming the completed reservation. You can manage the reservation, if needed, and download a voucher or send it to E-mail.
