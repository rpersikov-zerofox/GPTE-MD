# Search & Book of Car Rent Services

Search&Book of car rent includes the following steps:

## Search

To search for a car, in the search form, specify the following information.

1.  **Basic search options:** 1. **Pick-up point:** select the required destination from the list of destinations. Besides the city, it can be a place in a city, an airport, etc. 2. **Drop-off point:** specify the same information for the drop-off point. 3. **Date and time of pick-up and drop-off:** specify the date and the time of the pick-up and drop-off. 4. **Age:** specify the age of the driver.

![2024-11-05_13-56-57.jpg](/assets/2024-11-05_13-56-57.jpg)

2.  **Additional search options:** 1. **Number of passengers:** select the number of passengers. 2. **Only available:** select the check box in order to see only the offers that can be booked immediately and do not required extra supplier confirmation. 3. Specify suppliers type(s) to be considered while searching flights offers selecting **Internal suppliers** and/or **External suppliers**check boxes. 4. **Supplier:** select the preferred supplier. 5. **Citizenship:**select the driver's citizenship. 6. Select\*\* Show commission \*\*check box to display agency commission values at prices.

![2024-11-05_13-57-15.jpg](/assets/2024-11-05_13-57-15.jpg)

As soon as all the required search criteria are defined, click **Search** to initiate search process.

## Search results

Search results are arranged in the following way:

![2024-11-05_14-07-08.jpg](/assets/2024-11-05_14-07-08.jpg)

Browse through the list of results and click **Book** at the required offer.

For booking the service that suits your criteria the most, use the following prompts:

- Click Show description to view the detailed information on the vehicle.
- Click **Show on map** to navigate vehicle location on map.
- Review available services for every offer.

In order to filter the results and leave only those that suit your criteria completely, you can use the Filter form on the left part of the screen.

## Booking

Specify the information for the following sections:

- Offers
- Driver
- Pick-up and Drop-off points
- Confirmation

**Offers** Choose the required offer and click **Book**.

![2024-11-05_14-08-51.png](/assets/2024-11-05_14-08-51.png)

**Driver** In the Driver section, fill in the information about a driver. The mandatory fields are marked with an asterisk (\*):

![2024-11-05_14-22-11.jpg](/assets/2024-11-05_14-22-11.jpg)

**Pick-up and Drop-off points**

In the Pick-up and drop-off points section, specify corresponding points (it is possible to select airport, accommodation, city center):

![2024-11-05_14-23-38.jpg](/assets/2024-11-05_14-23-38.jpg)

You can leave a comment to the car service provider in the Comment field. It is possible to enter additional requests there (for example inquire about the availability of a safety seat for children)

![2024-11-05_14-25-04.png](/assets/2024-11-05_14-25-04.png)

**Extra Services**

In the Extra Services section, already included services are displayed, additionally other services can be selected if needed:

![2024-11-05_14-26-55.png](/assets/2024-11-05_14-26-55.png)

Click **Next step** to proceed to the Confirmation stage

### **Confirmation**

Specify the payment method selecting a preferred one from the available options.

Read the cancellation terms and select the respective check boxes and click\*\* Book:\*\*

![2024-11-05_14-28-56.jpg](/assets/2024-11-05_14-28-56.jpg)

## **Voucher**

After you click Book on Reservation confirmation page, a Reservation page opens with a pop-up confirming the completed reservation. You can manage the reservation, if needed, and download a voucher or send it to E-mail.
