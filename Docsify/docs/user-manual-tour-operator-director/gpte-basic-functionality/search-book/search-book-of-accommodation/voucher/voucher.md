# Voucher

**Reservation details**

After you click Book on Reservation confirmation page, a Reservation page opens with a pop-up confirming the completed reservation:

![2024-11-04_14-24-33.jpg](/assets/2024-11-04_14-24-33.jpg)

- Click **OK** in order to continue editing the reservation.

At this point you can manage the reservation and download a voucher / send it to E-mail.

Before finalizing the reservation and printing a voucher, some modifications can be executed:

- To edit the name of a reservation, click (Edit) at the reservation number. Enter the name of the reservation into the bar and save changes →

![2024-11-04_14-32-25.jpg](/assets/2024-11-04_14-32-25.jpg)

- To assign the reservation to other manager, click (Edit) opposite the Manager label →

![2024-11-04_14-36-29.jpg](/assets/2024-11-04_14-36-29.jpg)

- To change the reservation status, click (Edit) opposite the reservation status →

![2024-11-04_14-41-49.png](/assets/2024-11-04_14-41-49.png)

- To view the history of modifications made to the reservation, click **Manage reservation - View history of changes** →

![2024-11-04_14-41-55.png](/assets/2024-11-04_14-41-55.png)

**To get a voucher**:

![2024-11-04_14-44-42.png](/assets/2024-11-04_14-44-42.png)

- to download it in PDF format, either click **Voucher** in the reservation summary or click **Voucher** button at a particular service;
- to send it to E-mail, click **Voucher** button at a particular service, select "Send to E-mail" option → click OK.

![2024-11-04_14-45-51.png](/assets/2024-11-04_14-45-51.png)
