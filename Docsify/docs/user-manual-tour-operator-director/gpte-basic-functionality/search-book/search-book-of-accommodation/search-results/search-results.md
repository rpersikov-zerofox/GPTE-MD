# Search Results

On the Search results page, all the offers fitting your search criteria are displayed:

![2024-11-04_13-36-07.jpg](/assets/2024-11-04_13-36-07.jpg)

To choose the most suitable offer and proceed with the booking, use the following prompts:

1.  Click on a hotel name in search results page to get detailed hotel description (hotel information page will be opened):

![2024-11-04_13-54-18.jpg](/assets/2024-11-04_13-54-18.jpg)

1.  Click "View room offers" to see the list of room and meal types available in the hotel.
2.  Click "Show on map" to view the hotel location on the map.
3.  Explore the icons, standing for available amenities for each hotel, displayed on the hotel card.

**Compare hotels**

To compare offered hotels on the search results page,

1.  Click **Compare Hotels**:

![image-20201003-204937.jpg](/assets/image-20201003-204937.jpg)

1.  Click on offer to select it for comparison (check offer check box):

![image-20201003-205534.jpg](/assets/image-20201003-205534.jpg)

1.  In the appeared window select the format of saving the comparison results (PDF, Excel or Word document) OR click on **Send to E-mail** icon where the required format and email address are to be indicated:

![image-20201003-205715.png](/assets/image-20201003-205715.png)

## Tips on search

Important notes related to products (Tips functionality) was extended with the possibility to view/read the tips prior to booking.

The TO1 staff is able to:

- receive Tips related to the product/service during the search of a single product
- view Tips (internal notes) that are related to the product as the component of a Package

Tips are now also available for all products on search results, on product page, on the booking page.
