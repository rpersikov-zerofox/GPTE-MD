## **Filter results**

Sometimes the number of found offers may be quite redundant. In order to filter the results and leave only those that suit your criteria completely, you can use the Filter form on the left part of the screen:

![2024-11-28_16-03-25.png](/assets/2024-11-28_16-03-25.png)

You can apply the following filters:

- **Price**: move the sliders across the scale or indicate the required range manually to limit the permissible price range.
- **Date**: select a concrete date from the range.
- **Name**: enter the required tour name.
- **Short Description**: enter the required tour short description.
