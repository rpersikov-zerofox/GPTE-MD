# Search form

To search for products and services using the search form,

1.  On the Home page, in the Search form, click the tab with the product of your choice:

![2024-11-04_10-24-57.jpg](/assets/2024-11-04_10-24-57.jpg)

1.  The tabs stand for the services available for search. Each tab is marked with an icon indicating the travel service. *Please keep in mind that the set of tabs displayed in the figure above is used for illustrative purposes. The set of tabs depends on the quantity of services available for sale and thus differs from client to client.*
2.  In case there is no required product displayed, click **icon**. The set of products expands:

![2024-11-04_10-27-17.png](/assets/2024-11-04_10-27-17.png)

1.  Depending on the selected product, the search form changes. Further steps of searching procedure will be described in the subsections. They are arranged depending on the travel product.
