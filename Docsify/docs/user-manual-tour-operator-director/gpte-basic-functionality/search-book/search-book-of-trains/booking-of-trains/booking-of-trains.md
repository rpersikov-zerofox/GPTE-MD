# Booking of Trains

1.  On **Offers**section you can choose the coach type. Click the necessary radio-button opposite the required coach. Click **Continue**.

![Screenshot 2020-11-06 at 12.26.27-20201106-102808.jpg](/assets/Screenshot-2020-11-06-at-12.26.27-20201106-102808.jpg)

2.  At this step you can reserve available seats too. Click (Information icon) on the **Coach** (See group 1 in the figure below) or the **Availability** (See group 2 in the figure below.) to choose the seat. On The **Detailed info** window you can select seats by schema to by parameters:

- To select **by schema** click the vacant seat (should get the red color) and click **Book**.

![image-20201106-102246.jpg](/assets/image-20201106-102246.jpg)

- To select **by parameters** go to this tab and choose necessary coach number, service class and other parameters to proceed with booking. Click **Book** to continue.

![image-20201106-102252.jpg](/assets/image-20201106-102252.jpg)

3.  On **Passengers** section you should fill in passenger information.

![image-20201106-102443.jpg](/assets/image-20201106-102443.jpg)

On the Passengers stage, fill in the tourist data. The mandatory fields include:

- First name;
- Middle name;
- Last name;
- Date of birth;
- Tariff;
- Document type, number, and the state that issued the document.

4.  Click **Continue** to proceed the booking.
5.  On the **Seat selection and reservation** section you can Leave a comment for the service provider (optionally, e.g. request Extra services or inquire any additional information).

![image-20201106-102852.jpg](/assets/image-20201106-102852.jpg)

6.  Select payment method and Read tour operator conditions and select the respective check box.
7.  Click **Book.**

After you click **Book** you will be redirected to the reservation page where you can view the reservation details, add services to the reservation and manage your reservation. For more information, please see [Voucher](/wiki/spaces/GPTEUG/pages/968229072).
