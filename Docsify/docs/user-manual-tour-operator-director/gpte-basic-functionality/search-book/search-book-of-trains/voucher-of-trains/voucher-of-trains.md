# Voucher of Trains

1.  After you click Book the Contract comments window appears:

![image-20201016-122535.png](/assets/image-20201016-122535.png)

2.  To proceed the booking click **Continue** or click **Cancel** to undo this step and stay on the booking stage.
3.  In case you click **Continue**, you see **the train booking confirmation** window:

![image-20201016-130638.jpg](/assets/image-20201016-130638.jpg)

Here you can\*\* Issue ticket\*\* or **Cancel booking**. If you don't want to choose any of these options and want to see the final stage of booking click **Close**. 4. On the final stage of booking you can manage and edit the reservation.

![image-20201106-103418.jpg](/assets/image-20201106-103418.jpg)

For more information please see [Reservations](/wiki/spaces/GPTEUG/pages/1088389346).
