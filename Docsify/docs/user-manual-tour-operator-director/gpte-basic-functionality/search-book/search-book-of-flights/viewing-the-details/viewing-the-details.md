# Viewing the details

**Flight details** To view the detailed information about a flight, click Detailed Info. Detailed flight information appears below:

![2024-11-04_16-06-34.jpg](/assets/2024-11-04_16-06-34.jpg)

**Baggage information** To view the information on the baggage, refer to Baggage allowances section of the offer:

![2024-11-04_16-07-23.jpg](/assets/2024-11-04_16-07-23.jpg)

**Viewing client price breakdown** To view the breakdown of the client price, click the information icon at the Client price:

![2024-11-04_16-07-57.png](/assets/2024-11-04_16-07-57.png)

The Detailed price window appears:

![2024-11-04_16-08-09.png](/assets/2024-11-04_16-08-09.png)

**Viewing Fare rules** To view the Fare rules of an offer, click Fare rules. Pop-up with fare rules information appears:

![2024-11-04_16-12-16.jpg](/assets/2024-11-04_16-12-16.jpg)
