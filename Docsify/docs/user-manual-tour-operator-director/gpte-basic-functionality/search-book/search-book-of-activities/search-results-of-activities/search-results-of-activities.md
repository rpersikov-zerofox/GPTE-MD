- To see the detailed information on the offers, click **Show.**Available offers are expanded:

![2024-11-05_15-31-28.jpg](/assets/2024-11-05_15-31-28.jpg)

- Click the (Info sign) to view the detailed information on the activity.

**Saving Search Results to PDF**

**"Save offers" functionality is currently available via administration panel.**

To save search results to a PDF-file, select the required offers and click . The results will be downloaded to your computer as a PDF-file.
