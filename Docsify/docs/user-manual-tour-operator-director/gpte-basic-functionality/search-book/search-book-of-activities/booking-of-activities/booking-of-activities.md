# Booking of Activities

Booking accommodation consists of 3 steps:

## **Offers**

After you click **Book** on the Search results page, the Activity description page with the offers appears:

![2024-11-05_14-41-43.jpg](/assets/2024-11-05_14-41-43.jpg)

Select the preferred offer and proceed to booking.

## **Guests**

On the Guests page, enter the information about guests.

Add a comment to the supplier in the Comment field and click **Next step**:

![2024-11-05_15-38-24.jpg](/assets/2024-11-05_15-38-24.jpg)

## **Confirmation**

On the Confirmation stage:

Specify the payment method selecting a preferred one from the available options.

Select the terms of use check boxes and click\*\* Book\*\*:

![2024-11-05_15-41-16.jpg](/assets/2024-11-05_15-41-16.jpg)
