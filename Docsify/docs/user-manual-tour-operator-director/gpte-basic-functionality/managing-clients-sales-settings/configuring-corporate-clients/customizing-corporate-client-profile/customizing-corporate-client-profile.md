# Customizing Corporate Client Profile

**This functionality is currently available via administration panel.**

On the Settings tab, you can personalize the look of the system so that it complies with the brand and identity of a corporate client. You can also configure the localization preferences, links with external applications, and notifications.

To adjust the system to the look and feel of a corporate client,

1.  On the Navigation menu, point to **Clients**, and click **Corporate clients**.
2.  Click the corporate client you would like to configure and go to the **Settings** tab.
3.  On the settings tab, click **Edit**.
4.  Specify the information, according to the principles, described in .
5.  Click **Save.**
