# Browsing Corporate Clients

After you have created the profiles for corporate clients, you can:

- search for corporate clients by the set of available search parameters
- view the list of corporate users and offices
- edit company data

You can search for corporate clients using the search form at the top of the Clients page:

![2024-10-29_14-43-14.jpg](/assets/2024-10-29_14-43-14.jpg)

**To search for a corporate client,**

1.  On the Navigation bar, click My Company → Corporate clients.
2.  Select the items of your choice from the drop-down lists. You can search by one search parameter, combining several of them or by all the search parameters. In the latter case however make sure, you fill in the search form with correct data, otherwise the search results may be few or none.
3.  Click Search. If you get zero results, it means that there are no corporate clients matching your search criteria.

**To view the list of corporate clients of your company,**

1.  On the Navigation bar, click My Company → Corporate clients. The Clients page appears.
2.  On the Clients page, the table features general information about every corporate client including a company code, type, location, contact details, registration date and current status. *By default the entries in the list are arranged by alphabetical reservation.*
