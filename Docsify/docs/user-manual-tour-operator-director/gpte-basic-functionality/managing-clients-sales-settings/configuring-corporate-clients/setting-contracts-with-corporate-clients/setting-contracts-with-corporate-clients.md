# Setting Contracts with Corporate Clients

**This functionality is currently available via administration panel.**

To configure the contract terms that regulate the relationship between your company and a corporate client,

1.  On the Navigation menu, point to **Clients**, click **Corporate clients** and then click the client client for which you need to configure the contract terms.
2.  Click the **Contract** tab, and then click **Edit** to switch to the editing mode.
3.  Configure the terms of contract as described in [Managing Sales Settings](https://gp-team.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPTEUG/pages/1922249700) .
4.  Click **Save**.

In order to create reservations, your client must log in the system and appoint a direct sales manager.

According to specific settings of your installation version, you can define the currency that is used for settlements with the client company: the supplier\'s currency or the contract one. Depending on this parameter chosen, the prices are shown in the specified currency on the search and book forms, and on the Reservations page.
