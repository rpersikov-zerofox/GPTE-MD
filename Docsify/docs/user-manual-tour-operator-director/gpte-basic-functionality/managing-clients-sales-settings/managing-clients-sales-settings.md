# Managing Clients & Sales Settings

Build and manage your distribution network by creating client profiles for agencies, corporate clients, and private travelers. Configure pricing, commissions, and contracts to control how you sell travel products through different channels.

## Overview

The Managing Clients module provides tools to:

- Create and manage agency partnerships
- Set up corporate client accounts
- Configure private client profiles
- Define pricing and commission structures
- Manage contracts and agreements
- Control access and permissions for clients

## Client Types

GP Travel Enterprise supports multiple client categories, each with specific features:

### Agencies (Tour Operators & Travel Agencies)

Distribution partners who resell your travel products:

- [Configuring Agencies](configuring-agencies/configuring-agencies.md) - Set up agency partnerships
- Agency-specific pricing and commission structures
- User access control for agency staff
- Contract management and terms

### Corporate Clients

Business travel accounts with specific policies and approval workflows:

- [Configuring Corporate Clients](configuring-corporate-clients/configuring-corporate-clients.md) - Set up corporate accounts
- Travel policy enforcement
- Approval hierarchies
- Corporate-specific pricing and terms
- Expense tracking and reporting

### Private Clients

Individual travelers and consumers:

- [Configuring Individual Clients](configuring-individual-clients/configuring-individual-clients.md) - Manage personal travel accounts
- Direct consumer bookings
- Loyalty program management
- Personal pricing and promotions

## Client Management Workflow

### Setting Up a New Client

1. **Select Client Type** - Choose agency, corporate, or private client
2. **Enter Basic Information** - Company name, contact details, address
3. **Configure Pricing** - Set markups, commissions, and special rates
4. **Create User Accounts** - Add users for the client organization
5. **Set Permissions** - Define access levels and restrictions
6. **Activate Account** - Enable the client for bookings

### Managing Existing Clients

Common client management tasks:

- Update contact information and business details
- Modify pricing and commission structures
- Add or remove user accounts
- Review booking history and performance
- Generate client-specific reports
- Deactivate or suspend accounts

## Distribution Network

Build your sales channels:

1. **Direct Sales** - Sell directly to private clients through your website or office
2. **Agency Network** - Distribute through travel agencies and tour operators
3. **Corporate Accounts** - Manage business travel programs
4. **Multi-Level Distribution** - Create agency hierarchies with sub-agents
