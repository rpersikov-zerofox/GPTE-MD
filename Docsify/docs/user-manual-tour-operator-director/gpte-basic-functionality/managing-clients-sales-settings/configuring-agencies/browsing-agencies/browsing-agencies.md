# Browsing Agencies

After you have created the profiles for partner agencies, you can:

- search for agencies by the set of available search parameters;
- view the list of client agencies;
- edit client company data;
- manage system access of the client agencies.

You can search for agencies by a company code, type, name, location, period of registration, manager name using the search form at the top of the Clients page:

![2024-10-29_12-17-06.jpg](/assets/2024-10-29_12-17-06.jpg)

To search for a client agency,

1.  On the Navigation bar, click **My Company** →\*\*Agencies/Distributors\*\*.
2.  Select the items of your choice from the drop-down lists. You can search by one search parameter, combining several of them or by all the search parameters. In the latter case however make sure, you fill in the search form with correct data, otherwise the search results may be few or none.
3.  Click **Filter**. If you get zero results, it means that there are no client agencies/distributors matching your search criteria.

To view the list of agencies of your company,

1.  on the Navigation bar, click **My Company** →\*\*Agencies/Distributors\*\*. The Clients page appears.
2.  On the Clients page, the table features general information about every agency including a company code, type, location, contact details, registration date and current status.

By default the entries in the list are arranged by alphabetical reservation. Besides, you can sort the entries in one of the following ways:

- by the names of the agencies in reverse reservation (Z-A);
- by the type of the agencies, arranging the tour operators and travel agencies in groups;
- by registration date;
- by status (active/inactive).

To sort the list by a certain criterion, click the header of corresponding column.

**Sorting functionality is currently available via administration panel.**
