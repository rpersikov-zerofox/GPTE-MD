# Managing Private Clients\' Reservations

You can view the history of private clients\' reservations made via B2C website.

**Viewing private clients\' reservations** To view the list of reservations of a particular private client,

1.  On the Navigation bar, click My Company → Private clients. The Clients page appears.
2.  On the Clients page, click the private client of your choice.
3.  In the profile of the selected private client, click Reservations to proceed to the Reservations tab.
4.  On the Reservations tab, the list of reservations made by the private client is displayed:

![2024-10-29_15-49-42.jpg](/assets/2024-10-29_15-49-42.jpg)

**Creating new reservations for private clients** To create a new reservation for a client (with the information already stored in the CRM system),

1.  On the Navigation bar, click **Private clients**. The Clients page appears.
2.  On the Clients page, click the private client of your choice.
3.  In the profile of the private client, click Reservations to proceed to the Reservations tab.
4.  On the Reservations tab, click **New reservation request**.
