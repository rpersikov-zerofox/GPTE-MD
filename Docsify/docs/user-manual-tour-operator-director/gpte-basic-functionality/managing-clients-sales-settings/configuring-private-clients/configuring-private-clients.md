# Configuring Private Clients

Private Clients -- are those who book travel services for their own usage (for themselves or their families or friends), so they are the final consumers of travel product. By using GP Travel Enterprise, you can manage such clients, store their profiles and make reservations for them.

Note that to the contrary to other clients it's not necessary to create private client profile in advance. You can just fill in travelers details at the moment of booking. On the other hand if you have repetitive clients it's recommended to fill in their details in advance and link all the reservations to corresponding accounts to have full analytics and history of previous trips of this client.
