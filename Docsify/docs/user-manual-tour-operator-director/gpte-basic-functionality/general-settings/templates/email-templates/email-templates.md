# Email Templates

For the typical e-mails it's necessary to have templates for quick creation of notifications and e-mails based on them.

## Create New Template

To create a new notification template,

1.  navigate to **General Settings** menu → **Templates**:

![2024-11-27_13-32-20.png](/assets/2024-11-27_13-32-20.png)

*Email templates* page is displayed →

1.  Select a **Recipient type** for the emails (Company, Supplier or Tourists Templates) and click **Add**. New email template page appears, complete the email template with corresponding information. To view detailed information on the list of dynamic placeholders, click on (question) icon next to **Save Changes** button:

![2024-11-27_13-46-13.jpg](/assets/2024-11-27_13-46-13.jpg)

![2024-11-27_14-57-42.png](/assets/2024-11-27_14-57-42.png)

1.  Click **Save changes**. The created template is saved to database and is available for e-mails from Reservations and mass mails from all parts of the system.

To edit a template, click on icon in *Edit* column at the corresponding template.

There is a possibility to copy existing template to a new one clicking on **Copy** link, a copy of existing template will be created.

## Use Templates

The templates can be used in the following areas:

1.  **Mass mail for clients**(menu *My company -\> Private Clients, or My company -\> Corporate Clients, or My company -\> Travel agencies*\*\* \*\*etc. - ex. to send Birthday Congratulations):
2.  **Mass mail for reservations** (e.g. Flight time has been changed - menu *Reservations*):
3.  Manual e-mail from **Reservation** (e.g. send again booking confirmation to supplier in case he says that did not receive previous one - see **Send e-mail** button within *Reservation Details* - both in summary and for each service):

How to use templates in e-mails:

- by default e-mail is empty, template is not selected → select a client type (check checkboxes "client", "supplier", "traveler") to indicate an e-mail recipient. If no checkbox is selected, then all e-mail templates from settings are available within **Template** drop-down. If only some of these checkboxes are selected, then only templates that relate to corresponding client type (ex. "Supplier E-mail Templates" from settings) are available.
- When a template is selected, **Subject** and **Message** fields of the e-mail are automatically filled.
- Fill in sender's e-mail and add more recipient(s) if needed.
- Attach file(s) in **Attachments** section if needed.
- Click **Send** or **Preview** to verify e-mail displaying before sending it.

## Selection of templates

When a new e-mail is being sent from the system, it's based on certain e-mail template that can be configured via CMS. Now it is possible to select from templates for manual sending of e-mails - both via Reservation details, from the list of Reservations and from the lists of Clients.

![2024-11-27_15-00-44.jpg](/assets/2024-11-27_15-00-44.jpg)

## Own templates for e-mails

One more useful enhancement - is ability to create own e-mail templates and use them for e-mails.

For example, you can configure template for Birthday congratulations or New Destinations Proposals, or any other reasons. As usual, you can also use placeholders within templates for flexibility.
