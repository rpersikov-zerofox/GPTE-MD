# Company Profile

Your company profile forms the foundation of your GP Travel Enterprise system configuration. Proper setup ensures accurate information appears throughout the platform in invoices, vouchers, reports, and client communications.

## Overview

Configure your company profile through three main areas:

1. **Company Information** - Set up basic business details, contact information, and legal documentation
2. **User Management** - Create and manage user accounts with appropriate roles and permissions
3. **Profile Settings** - Configure system preferences, localization, API access, and platform customization

Each section contains specific configuration options that control how your company operates within the platform.

## Getting Started

Complete your company profile setup in order:

1. [Configure Company Information](configuring-information-about-the-company/configuring-information-about-the-company.md) - Enter business details and upload required documents
2. [Manage Company Users](managing-company-users/managing-company-users.md) - Create user accounts and assign roles
3. [Specify Profile Settings](specifying-company-profile-settings/specifying-company-profile-settings.md) - Customize system behavior and preferences

**Note:** Some features may be unavailable until you complete all required company profile fields.
