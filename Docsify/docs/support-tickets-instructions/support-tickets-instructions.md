# SUPPORT TICKETS INSTRUCTIONS

We hope the information below will be useful for you and we will be glad to provide you the best maintenance services.

**How to create a Jira ticket to best address your issue?**

1.  Choose *General*and than the issue type: *Change Request / Question / System Error*
2.  *Summary*-- please, indicate the essence of the issue
3.  *Priority --*please, indicate the priority of the issue taking into account *Technical support and maintenance agreement p.4 Defects priority and response time*
4.  *Description --*in case of *System Error*, please describe all the necessary details of the issue, as in the template below.
5.  *Attachment --*please, take and send us screenshots or screen recordings of the issue.

**System Error Description Template:**

- Link and Environment (LIVE or TEST, browser version, device for mobile/application issues)
- User role (guest / director / supervisor / corporate client etc)
- Date and Time when the issue occurred
- Steps to reproduce: Selfoperated products \> Hotels \> Hillton \> Daily rates and change the prices for period DD.MM.YY - DD.MM.YY
- Result: the prices haven\'t changed in the table
- Expected result: the prices changed and displayed correctly
- Screenshots or screen recordings of the issue
