---
title: Extended CRM
---

Extended CRM gives the ability to combine the online booking engine and management of relationships with your clients within single platform. With Extended CRM set of modules you can:

* Classify your clients
* Track opportunities with potential clients
* Manage marketing campaigns
* Keep history of communications with clients
* View overall analytics on clients

**Loyalty** - A special marketing tool that helps a company to establish long-term relationships with its customers and to turn them into frequent buyers.

**Classifications** - Mark your clients as VIP / high / low / normal priority. Classify your clients depending on their industry and company size.

**Opportunities** - Handle "big deals" with potential clients. Participate in tenders for corporate travel management or hold negotiations with large Agency chains.

**Campaigns** - Manage marketing campaigns. Plan what steps towards gaining new clients you're going to undertake, what is the allocated budget and who is responsible.

**History of Communications** - Record all calls and meetings held with your clients. Plan and send arbitrary e-mails to your clients directly from the system.

**Analytics** - Gives you full overview of your clients including average trip budget, preferred locations, categories and more.
