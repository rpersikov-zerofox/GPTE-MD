#### Viewing News <a href="#viewing-news" id="viewing-news"></a>

**To view the list of news** that have already been published and modify them,

1.  On the General settings menu, click News:

    ![](blob:https://gp-team.atlassian.net/e72b88ef-cb10-4b4c-84ed-c457057db8ce#media-blob-url=true\&id=6873af2a-0674-470b-a9ca-39391a0799e6\&collection=contentId-845185446\&contextId=1922255831\&mimeType=image%2Fpng\&name=image-20200921-105250.png\&size=37298\&width=295\&height=489\&alt=)
2. On the News page.
   1. To view a piece of news, click title (_see group 1 in the figure below)._
   2. To edit a piece of news, click title and then in the Message window, click Edit.
   3.  To send a piece of news via e-mail, click its title and then in the Message window, click Send the news by e-mail.

       ![](blob:https://gp-team.atlassian.net/15abe039-082e-4761-b097-abad55e1cf7f#media-blob-url=true\&id=588f4ccf-26ef-484b-9d9d-7c755e57a3a3\&collection=contentId-845185446\&contextId=1922255831\&mimeType=image%2Fpng\&name=image-20220513-114236.png\&size=5516\&width=472\&height=157\&alt=)
   4. To deactivate a piece of news (_so that is not displayed on the Home page but remains in the news directory with the possibility of further restoration_):
      1. click the news title (_see group 1 in the figure below_)
      2. click Edit to switch to editing mode
      3. clear the Active check box.
   5. To delete a piece of news, click delete opposite the required entry (_see group 2 in the figure below_).
   6.  To change the order of displaying, click the arrow opposite the required item to pull it up or down (see group 3 in the figure below).

       ![](blob:https://gp-team.atlassian.net/7707f4a6-f60f-412b-97ba-2e320eca0bfe#media-blob-url=true\&id=49bd26c3-69aa-4e89-bc6c-33356fd50806\&collection=contentId-845185446\&contextId=1922255831\&mimeType=image%2Fpng\&name=image-20200923-111542.png\&size=90544\&width=1174\&height=231\&alt=)

#### Creating News <a href="#creating-news" id="creating-news"></a>

**To create a news article** to display on the Home page,

1. On the General settings menu, click News.
2.  On the News page, click Create. The New message window appears:

    ![](blob:https://gp-team.atlassian.net/be0c4166-5742-4142-a1a9-28c46c246ec7#media-blob-url=true\&id=26f9f937-ea99-494a-b91d-42ea221b26b5\&collection=contentId-845185446\&contextId=1922255831\&mimeType=image%2Fpng\&name=image-20200921-110130.png\&size=44435\&width=874\&height=550\&alt=)
3. Click the required language to expand the tab.
4. Enter the title of the message and the text itself.
5. Set the availability of the message:
   1. **For all**: the news is displayed to all system users, including clients of your partners.
   2. **For clients**: the news is displayed to all direct clients of your company. The news is not displayed to the clients of your partners.
   3. **For me**: the news is displayed only to the users of your company.
   4.  **Selected sales channels**: the news is displayed to the clients whom you manually select.\
       To add a client, click Add. The Sales channel window appears. From the drop-down, select the required sales channel and select the check boxes opposite the required clients. Click Save.

       ![](blob:https://gp-team.atlassian.net/779af715-6073-4221-af88-bcf113407248#media-blob-url=true\&id=c860e18f-d3ca-4185-bbb7-e7abd75b2923\&collection=contentId-845185446\&contextId=1922255831\&mimeType=image%2Fpng\&name=image-20220513-114804.png\&size=9796\&width=249\&height=395\&alt=)

       &#x20;
6. Click Save.
7. Click Close to return to the list of news.

#### Viewing the List of Links <a href="#viewing-the-list-of-links" id="viewing-the-list-of-links"></a>

To view the list of links that have already been published and modify them,

1. On the General settings menu, click Links.
2.  On the Links page,

    1. To view or edit a link, click its title (_see group 1 in the figure below_).
    2.  To deactivate a link:

        1. click its title
        2. click Edit
        3. clear the Active check box.

        The deactivated links are not deleted permanently, they are moved to archive with the possibility of further restoration.
    3. To go to the web-site connected to the link, click the link in the Content column (_see group 2 in the figure below_).
    4.  To delete a link, click delete opposite the required entry (_see group 3 in the figure below_).

        ![](blob:https://gp-team.atlassian.net/11e4c2db-e4a2-417b-9852-e7c5fee1e472#media-blob-url=true\&id=4b1efb03-024a-4c4f-b5dd-d2f1e9823814\&collection=contentId-845185446\&contextId=1922255831\&mimeType=image%2Fpng\&name=image-20200923-121502.png\&size=168043\&width=1222\&height=278\&alt=)
