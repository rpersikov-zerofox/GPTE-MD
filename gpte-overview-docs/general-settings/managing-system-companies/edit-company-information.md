# Edit Company Information

If you need access a particular company in order to

* Add a new employee
* Upload new documents
* Modify companies' contracts
* Change look & feel
* Configure corporate policy
* View or edit companies' reservations, etc.



1. On the General settings menu, click All companies.
2. The Companies page appears. On the Companies page, choose the required company and click its name. The Company window appears:

![2024-11-06\_15-14-56.png](blob:https://gp-team.atlassian.net/b64a08d6-4bee-4fd0-8b30-c42b2d20b54e#media-blob-url=true\&id=bf699b92-c229-47db-b9fc-9443ba67a320\&collection=contentId-1922255538\&contextId=1922255538\&mimeType=image%2Fpng\&name=2024-11-06_15-14-56.png\&size=234402\&width=1398\&height=777\&alt=2024-11-06_15-14-56.png)

3. In the window, go to the required tab, make needed modifications and click Save.
