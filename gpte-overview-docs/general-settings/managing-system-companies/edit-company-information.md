# Edit Company Information

If you need access a particular company in order to

* Add a new employee
* Upload new documents
* Modify companies' contracts
* Change look & feel
* Configure corporate policy
* View or edit companies' reservations, etc.



1. On the General settings menu, click **All companies**.
2. On the Companies page, choose a company and click its name. The Company window appears:



<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/mng comp 2.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Choose a tab, make edits and click **Save**.
