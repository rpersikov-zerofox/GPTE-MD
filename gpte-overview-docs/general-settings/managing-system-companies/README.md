# Managing System Companies

* [Search Companies](search-companies.md)
* [Edit Company Information](edit-company-information.md)
* [Deactivate Companies](deactivate-companies.md)
* [View Archived Companies](view-archived-companies.md)

On the **Companies** page, you can view the list of companies that have ever been created in the system. The list of companies may include:

* Travel agencies
* Tour operators
* Suppliers
* Groups of companies
* Corporate clients

Pay attention however that the list of companies is highly specific and depends on several factors, specifically,

* the network of travel agencies
* the network of suppliers
* the availability of direct contracted suppliers
* corporate clients, etc.
