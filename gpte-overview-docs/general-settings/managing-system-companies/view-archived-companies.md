# View Archived Companies

To view the list of archived companies:

1. On the General settings menu, click **All companies**.&#x20;
2. On the Companies page, switch off the **Active** toggle:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/mng comp arch.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Now the list contains only archive companies. To return to the standard display mode, switch the toggle back to **Active**.
