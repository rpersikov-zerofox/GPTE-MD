# View Archived Companies

To view the list of archived companies:

1. On the General settings menu, click All companies. The Companies page appears.
2. On the Companies page, switch off the Active toggle:

![2024-11-06\_15-18-27.png](blob:https://gp-team.atlassian.net/77472499-bbf4-40f8-a6dc-5de26919c0ef#media-blob-url=true\&id=d49c79c5-8fb0-4f96-95da-84b80606e22f\&collection=contentId-1922255538\&contextId=1922255538\&mimeType=image%2Fpng\&name=2024-11-06_15-18-27.png\&size=66653\&width=1392\&height=179\&alt=2024-11-06_15-18-27.png)
