# Search Companies

To view the list of companies registered in the system:

1. On the **General settings** menu, click **All companies**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/mng comp 1.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. The **Companies** page appears.
3. If the number of companies is sufficient, use the Filter form to filter for a particular company:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/mng comp filter (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Fill in the following search criteria:
   1. **Type:** select the type of the company you search for (e.g. travel agency, tour operator, etc.)
   2. **Country:** select the country where the company is registered.
   3. **City:** select the city where the company is registered.
   4. **Name:** if you search for a particular company, enter its name.
   5. **Registration:** define the period when the company was registered in the system.
   6. **Company code/Alias:** enter the company code, which is ascribed by the system automatically upon creating a company profile or an alias as its replacement.
   7. **Click Filter.** The list of companies is contracted to those that suit your search parameters.

Note: you can download the list of companies with the general information about them to your computer as an Excel file. For that, on the Companies page, click Excel icon. The list of companies will be downloaded to your computer.
