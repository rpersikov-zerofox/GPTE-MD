# Search Companies

To view the list of companies registered in the system:

1. On the **General settings** menu, click **All companies**.

![2024-11-06\_15-10-02.png](blob:https://gp-team.atlassian.net/2d7e0d21-a440-4ce0-855f-9e1f7b468148#media-blob-url=true\&id=c8207ad6-0815-4c2e-bf8e-e9446ddb102f\&collection=contentId-1922255538\&contextId=1922255538\&mimeType=image%2Fpng\&name=2024-11-06_15-10-02.png\&size=43273\&width=185\&height=315\&alt=2024-11-06_15-10-02.png)

2. The **Companies** page appears.
3. If the number of companies is sufficient, use the Filter form to filter for a particular company:

![2024-11-06\_15-10-56.png](blob:https://gp-team.atlassian.net/b3ca1755-cbfd-4870-9205-e622a61f3ec4#media-blob-url=true\&id=d617dc67-b49e-4a98-a5f8-de13ddc8397f\&collection=contentId-1922255538\&contextId=1922255538\&mimeType=image%2Fpng\&name=2024-11-06_15-10-56.png\&size=34923\&width=710\&height=254\&alt=2024-11-06_15-10-56.png)

4. Fill in the following search criteria:
   1. **Type:** select the type of the company you search for (e.g. travel agency, tour operator, etc.)
   2. **Country:** select the country where the company is registered.
   3. **City:** select the city where the company is registered.
   4. **Name:** if you search for a particular company, enter its name.
   5. **Registration:** define the period when the company was registered in the system.
   6. **Company code/Alias:** enter the company code, which is ascribed by the system automatically upon creating a company profile or an alias as its replacement.
   7. **Click Filter.** The list of companies is contracted to those that suit your search parameters.

Note: you can download the list of conpanies with the genatal information about them to your computer as an Excel file. For that, on the Companies page, click ![2024-11-06\_15-12-40.png](blob:https://gp-team.atlassian.net/2f0e6d33-5935-4158-8393-3cdbb7bc921f) . The list of companies will be downloaded to your computer.
