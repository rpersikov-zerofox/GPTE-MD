# Processing Registration Requests

Companies that are interested in cooperation with you can leave a registration request.

**To view the list of the companies** pending registration on the **My company** menu click Leads / Registration requests.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/mng leads 1.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To view more information about a company, click its name in the list.

{% hint style="info" %}
You can contact the company representative using the contact details from the registration request form to sign a contract and grant access to the system according to the standard registration procedure.
{% endhint %}

**To provide the company with system access:**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/mng lead 2.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Click **Activate** opposite the required company in the list.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/mng lead 3.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. The Confirmation window appears.
3. Click **Confirm**.
